Stay on top of payment deadlines

h3>

Payment Due Login Popup gives accounting users a focused reminder of posted customer invoices and vendor bills that are due or overdue and still have a residual balance.

p>
div>
div>
section>

i> Login reminder

The reminder is shown once per Odoo login session and does not interrupt every browser refresh.

div>

i> Clear payment list

Review sales invoices and purchase bills with partner, company, due date, status, and amount due.

div>

i> Quick navigation

Open a document directly while the reminder stays available in a compact minimized box.

div>
div>
section>

Included behavior

  • Only posted customer invoices and vendor bills are included.
  • Only documents due today or earlier with a positive residual amount are included.
  • Closing the reminder hides it for the current login session, including browser refreshes.
  • Logging out clears the dismissal state so the reminder appears after the next login.
  • Minimizing keeps the reminder available without blocking normal Odoo work.
  • Data is fetched using the current user's normal access rights and allowed companies.

section>

Odoo 19.0 with the Accounting application installed.
